# Fitness Offsets

I am processing raw fitness (survival, length change, and biomass change) data from the 2024 eelgrass mesocosm experiments in order to calculate absolute and relative fitness, as well as absolute and relative common-garden/current-future fitness offsets.

## Current Status

I have already processed the survival and growth data into usable formats, and summarized this data by coast, treatment, and population. This will act as the absolute fitness data from which relative fitness and fitness offsets will be calculated. Note that the overall trends here are that survival is highest in the high salinity treatments (W coast treatments, regardless of temperature), while biomass and length are largely the same across treatments with a significant amount of variability. 

I will now start calculating fitness offsets - start with absolute fitness offsets, then calculate relative fitness/fitness offsets. Before I can do this, I need to figure out how to scale fitness by the environmental distance between home environments and treatments, since our treatments are averages of W/E coast conditions and do not actually represent the home environments of any given population.
